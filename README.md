# Customer Segmentation using K-Means Clustering

## Overview

This project applies the K-Means Clustering algorithm to segment mall customers based on their annual income and spending score. The goal is to identify different customer groups for better business understanding and targeted marketing strategies.

---

## Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Scikit-learn

---

## Project Workflow

1. Data Loading and Understanding
2. Data Cleaning and Missing Value Analysis
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Outlier and Correlation Analysis
5. Feature Selection and Scaling
6. Finding Optimal Clusters using Elbow Method
7. Training K-Means Clustering Model
8. Model Evaluation using Silhouette Score
9. Customer Segment Visualization
10. Business Insights and Conclusion

---

## Model Performance

* Optimal Clusters: 5
* Silhouette Score: 0.55

---

## Customer Segments

* High Income – High Spending
* High Income – Low Spending
* Low Income – High Spending
* Low Income – Low Spending
* Medium Income – Medium Spending

---

## Business Insights

* Premium customers can be targeted with loyalty programs.
* High income but low spending customers can be targeted with personalized marketing.
* Discount campaigns work well for low income high spending customers.
* Medium income customers form the major customer base.

---

## Conclusion

The K-Means clustering model successfully segmented customers into meaningful groups based on spending behavior and annual income. These insights can help businesses improve customer targeting and marketing strategies.
